# Gallery

## Overview
The gallery provides local indexing, filtering, and organization for photos and videos without hosting or altering your original file structure.

## Culling & File Organization
- **Ratings & Reject Culling**: Supports 1–5 star ratings and "Reject" flags. Quickly mark unwanted shots as "Reject" during culling, then filter and batch-delete them using the top toolbar.
- **Drag-and-Drop Organization**: Drag selected photo or video cards to the left folder list to move files, or to the tag bar to assign tags.
- **Clipboard Integration**: Copy files directly to the system clipboard for quick pasting and sharing, or paste screenshots from the clipboard directly into the gallery.
- **Filtering & Views**: Supports Grid, Masonry, List, and other view modes, with flexible filtering by color, date, rating, tags, and more.
- **Network Storage Support**: Full support for network shared folders (UNC paths, such as NAS or LAN network drives).

## Details Panel
Select any photo or video to view dominant colors, resolution, file size, creation date, custom description, and color histogram in the right details panel.

## Infinity Nikki Extension

- **Camera Parameters**: Automatically parses photo shooting parameters, allowing you to copy camera parameter codes anytime for in-game import.
- **Dye Codes**: Record and copy outfit dye codes for easy outfit management and sharing.
- **Photo Map**: Pins photos onto the in-game map based on shot locations. Hover over pins for photo previews, or click cards to jump to the photo viewer.

## Data Safety & 30-Day Protection
- **Non-Intrusive Indexing**: Original files remain in place on your disk; the app only maintains a local metadata database and thumbnail cache.
- **30-Day Missing Grace Period**: If files are moved or renamed, associated tags, ratings, and descriptions are preserved for a 30-day grace period. Restoring files to their original paths during this time automatically re-links all metadata.
